# Weekly $750 Tracker

A weekly spending budget tracker. Weeks run Saturday to Friday and the budget ($750 by default) resets every Saturday. Every finished week at or under the budget banks the difference; a week over it subtracts. A hillside grove of three trees, one for each of the boys (Miles, Lucas and Julien, ages 1, 3 and 7), grows with every week under budget. Surprise fruit and flowers appear along the way, and the grove is fully grown at 12 weeks.

- **Add an expense** with an amount, date, category and note, or **log a no-spend day**.
- **This week:** what's spent and left of the budget, and how many days until it resets on Saturday.
- **Net saved**, money saved and overspent, weeks under budget and the current streak (finished weeks only).
- **Week by week:** each week's spending against the budget line. Tap a week to see or remove its entries.
- **Where it goes:** spending by category for this week, this month or all time.
- **Family sync:** sign in near the bottom of the page to share expenses between both phones (see [../FAMILY-SYNC.md](../FAMILY-SYNC.md)). Expenses added on both phones at the same time are all kept.
- **Backup:** download, restore or erase.

Open `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo>/expenses/` on the phone and **Add to Home Screen**. If you change any files after it's installed, bump `CACHE` in `sw.js`.

This app replaces the earlier Claude artifact version of the tracker.

This app started as the Daily $80 Tracker. Expenses logged in the daily version carry over and are grouped into Saturday-to-Friday weeks.
