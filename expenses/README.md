# Daily $80 Tracker

A daily spending allowance tracker. Every day logged at or under the allowance ($80 by default) banks the difference; a day over it subtracts. A hillside grove of three trees, one for each of the boys (Miles, Lucas and Julien, ages 1, 3 and 7), grows with every day under budget. Surprise fruit and flowers appear along the way, and the grove is fully grown at 30 days.

- **Add an expense** with an amount, date, category and note, or **log a no-spend day**.
- **Net saved**, money saved and overspent, days under budget and the current streak.
- **Day by day:** each day's spending against the allowance line. Tap a day to see or remove its entries.
- **Where it goes:** spending by category for the last 7 days, this month or all time.
- **Family sync:** sign in near the bottom of the page to share expenses between both phones (see [../FAMILY-SYNC.md](../FAMILY-SYNC.md)). Expenses added on both phones at the same time are all kept.
- **Backup:** download, restore or erase.

Open `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo>/expenses/` on the phone and **Add to Home Screen**. If you change any files after it's installed, bump `CACHE` in `sw.js`.

This app replaces the earlier Claude artifact version of the tracker.
