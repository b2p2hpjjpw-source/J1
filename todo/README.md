# Our To-Do List

A shared to-do list for the two of us, in the same look as the [Daily $80 Tracker](../expenses/). A tree on the hillside grows a piece of fruit for every task finished this week, and starts fresh each Monday.

- **Add a task** with who it's for (either of us, or one of us by name), a list (Home, Kids, Errands, Groceries, Money, Work, Other), an optional due date and a note.
- **To do** is grouped into Overdue, Today, Coming up and Anytime. Filter to one person's tasks (which also shows the "either of us" ones).
- **Tap the circle** to check a task off, or tap it again under Done to bring it back. **Tap a task** to edit it.
- **Our names:** tap *Our names* at the top to change "Mom" and "Dad".
- **Family sync:** sign in near the bottom of the page on both phones to share the list (see [../FAMILY-SYNC.md](../FAMILY-SYNC.md)). Tasks added on both phones at the same time are all kept.
- **Backup:** download, restore or erase.

Open `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo>/todo/` on each phone and **Add to Home Screen**. If you change any files after it's installed, bump `CACHE` in `sw.js`.
