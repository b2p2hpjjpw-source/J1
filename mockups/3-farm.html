Why it might work

  • Fewest decisions per screen, the easiest layout for a 3-year-old.
  • The buddy talks, and a progress bar shows how many steps are left.
  • “Not today” items stay visible so kids learn they exist.
  • A summary screen at the end, “Your breakfast!”, ends in a happy dance.

Trade-off

  • More taps for the 7-year-old, who may want a faster mode.
