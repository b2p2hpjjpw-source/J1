Breakfast Diner 🍳

A cheerful retro diner. Kids tap big menu cards (picture + word), see their choices land on a tray, then ring the bell to send the order. Works for both ages, and the 7-year-old can read the words.

Why it might work

  • Familiar “restaurant” game: kids love being the customer.
  • Tabs keep food, drinks and extras tidy.
  • Unavailable items get a grey ALL GONE sticker, so they stay visible but can't be tapped.
  • Ding! sound and confetti when the order is sent.

Trade-off

  • Tabs are a little abstract for a 3-year-old.
