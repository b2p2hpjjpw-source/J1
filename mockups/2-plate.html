Why it might work

  • Big faces and pictures, so no reading needed.
  • Dragging food onto a plate feels like play, not a form.
  • Items that aren't available are greyed out and “sleeping” (zzz), and they wiggle “no” if tapped.
  • Each child has their own colour and buddy.

Trade-off

  • Dragging takes more fine-motor skill, so tapping will work too.
